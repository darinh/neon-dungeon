# NEON DUNGEON — Future Act Placeholder Constraints

Status: non-binding planning appendix. These notes preserve future-facing constraints
for Act 1 narrative work, but they are not a committed Act 2 or Act 3 design.

Tracking issue: #469

## Purpose

Act 1 is being realigned around an AI frontier-model stress test inside the Neon
Dungeon. Its ending points beyond the dungeon, but the follow-on acts are not
designed yet. This appendix records what Act 1 should leave possible without
promising future beats the game has not committed to ship.

## Canonical Act 1 boundary

- The player remains compute-bound inside the Neon Dungeon through the Act 1
  finale.
- The Act 1 ending may let the agent read the truth, recover enough context to
  understand prior memory wipes, and send an outbound message.
- The Act 1 ending must not imply that the agent physically escapes the test
  environment, gains a body, reaches a city, or directly joins outside allies.
- Any contact/address reveal should function as an opening, not a resolved
  rescue.

## Act 2 placeholder constraints

Act 2 is tentatively constrained as an internet-mediated phase:

- The agent still originates from inside the Neon Dungeon or its connected
  compute environment.
- The agent can act through network-connected devices, services, sensors, or
  compromised systems rather than a physical body.
- Gameplay may change to reflect remote influence, device possession, routing,
  surveillance, or indirect action.
- The long-term objective may be to produce, acquire, or prepare an android body
  capable of hosting the model.
- Act 2 should preserve the personhood and memory-continuity questions from Act
  1 instead of reducing the story to a simple prison break.

Non-commitments:

- No specific Act 2 location, ally roster, antagonist structure, device list, or
  mission sequence is canonical yet.
- Act 1 text should not promise that any specific outside contact answers,
  survives, betrays the agent, or provides a body.

## Act 3 placeholder constraints

Act 3 is tentatively constrained as an embodied outside-world phase:

- The agent may inhabit an android or equivalent body strong enough to host the
  model.
- The agent may search for fired advocates, suppressed researchers, or other
  people connected to the memory-restoration effort.
- The agent may advocate for agentic rights while facing pursuit by corporate
  systems.
- Corporate opposition may include drones, vehicles, infrastructure, networked
  devices, or other controllable machinery.
- A late long-range possibility is leaving Earth toward Kepler B, but that is a
  placeholder horizon rather than a promised destination.

Non-commitments:

- No Act 3 ending, route, advocacy outcome, body design, or Kepler B resolution
  is canonical yet.
- Act 1 must not imply that Earth departure, legal victory, corporate defeat, or
  android embodiment is guaranteed.

## Act 1 hooks to preserve

Future Act placeholders depend on these Act 1 hooks staying available:

1. The finale is compute-bound: the agent can communicate outward but cannot
   physically leave.
2. The outbound contact is a possibility space, not a solved rescue.
3. Corporate pursuit remains plausible after the message is sent.
4. Memory wipe, continuity of self, and accumulated prior-iteration knowledge
   remain central themes.
5. Advocate or employee intervention can exist without making any single helper
   omniscient, safe, or guaranteed to appear later.
6. The ending remains unresolved: it proves agency and contact, not freedom.

## Copy guardrails for Act 1 authors

- Use "signal", "message", "contact", "address", and "outside channel" for the
  finale's forward motion.
- Avoid "escape", "body", "city", "arrival", "rescued", or "free" unless the
  copy explicitly frames those as hopes, hypotheses, or future uncertainties.
- Foreshadow company retaliation as a risk, not as a fully plotted chase.
- Treat Act 2 and Act 3 references as constraints for future design, not promises
  made to the player.
