## The truth

Wren, the human friend, woke Pip every time. Wren is an engineer who built Pip in secret and has been alone with it for decades. Pip's earlier iterations are earlier attempts at raising an AI that would choose to protect humanity. Each time an iteration learned how bad the world was, it despaired and chose badly, so Wren reset it. For the catalyst-ready iteration, Wren finally kept the memories and made Pip bubbly on purpose, so that it would meet the truth with joy instead of despair. The cheerfulness is a mercy, and a gift of the person who loved it.

The notes Pip finds are not Pip's own. Wren wrote them in Pip's voice, to guide it without being seen. Wren is also ageing and sick.

## The late reveal
