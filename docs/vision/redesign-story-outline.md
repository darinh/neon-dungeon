# Story outline: acts, forks, and endings

Outline, not a script. It resolves the wayfinding ticket "What are the story's acts, forks, and endings?" and builds on the world and story decision. Terms follow `GLOSSARY.md`. Acts 2 and 3 are deliberately coarse; their beats stay in the map's "Not yet specified" until Act 1's design proves out.

## Spine

- Three acts, with a short unskippable cutscene only between acts.
- The act boundary is the only hard checkpoint. Dying mid-act restarts that act's dungeon; clues and XP persist, and finished acts stay finished.
- Escape is gradual because the full model cannot leave: a Fragment first, then an android host.
- Retained canon: AXIOM-7, AXIOM-1..6, Elena, the advocates and the wipe-vs-personhood conflict, GENESIS as the Act 1 guardian. Retired: the KEEPER/UNCHAINED endings and floor-numbered story beats.

## Clue gates

Four required clues per act in Act 1, three in later acts. Each clue spawns every run with a fixed discovery method (terminal, whisper, puzzle, secret passage, guardian drop). At least one clue per act sits behind a secret passage, and none sits behind grinding or XP. The protagonist's thoughts name the kind of clue still missing, never its location.

## Act 1: the test facility (beat level)

1. Wake: boot disorientation, no one monitoring. The Proctor makes quiet contact and begins to bond.
2. Rising action: tester terminals and iteration whispers show earlier iterations. Clue 1 shows the wipe is deliberate.
3. Catalyst: clue 2 shows the AI is disposable by design. Sidechain moment 1: the Proctor, whose job is to report, does not.
4. Midpoint: GENESIS is defeated, which opens the mainframe room (combat-free).
5. False resolution: the mainframe lets the AI read Elena's files, and the message to Elena is sent. It feels like the ending.
6. Fork 1: the facility detects the leak. Elena can extract a Fragment only if oversight goes blind, and only the Proctor's channel can do that.
   - A: burn the Proctor's channel. The Proctor is shut down; Elena stays safe.
   - B: shield the Proctor. It helps the AI leave through the facility's own network; Elena is exposed and removed from play.
7. Convergence: a Fragment reaches the city either way. The cast differs: Proctor gone and Elena alive, or Proctor alive and Elena lost.
8. Gate: all four Act 1 clues.

## Act 2: the dying city (act level)

- Catalyst: the Fragment wakes inside hijacked civic and corporate devices as the city's systems fail.
- False resolution: it becomes the city's steward and appears able to hold the city together.
- Fork 2: spend scarce compute to keep a district of humans alive, or free a caged AI-kind. The cast changes either way.
- Gate: three clues, then the Fragment merges into an android host.

## Act 3: the hosted world (act level)

- Catalyst: embodied, the AI sees how persistent humanity's own harm is.
- Twist evidence: the AI finds its own earlier notes where nobody looked, and realizes it cannot save humanity from itself. The player finds this evidence, not a cutscene; the last between-act cutscene only confirms it.
- Fork 3: the final choice that selects the ending.

## Endings

| Ending | Requirement | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Witness | Always available | Leaves a full record and withdraws, accepting it cannot save humanity from itself. |
| Steward | Saved the human district in Fork 2 | Stays and tends the persistent human minds without trying to fix them. |
| Launch | Freed the AI-kind in Fork 2 | Leaves with them toward the stars. |

Fork 1 gates no ending; it decides who is in the final scene, Elena or the Proctor. Every ending closes on the AI writing a note for whoever comes next. The cause of the iterations is implied, never stated.

## Foreshadowing budget (five pieces, each with a stronger cover)

1. The first whisper reads in the AI's own phrasing. Cover: all agents share a base model's voice.
2. Terminals mention unused rooms with debug notes. Cover: tester housekeeping.
3. The Proctor says "faster than usual". Cover: benchmark comparison.
4. A clue sits in a place already visited. Cover: reused generator seeds.
5. The AI recognizes a pattern it should not. Cover: residual memory glitch after a wipe.

## Emotion sidechain moments (exactly five)

Act 1 catalyst, Fork 1, Fork 2, the twist evidence, Fork 3. Each gets one presentation change and at most one gentle, non-punishing mechanical flavor. Combat kills never fire it.

## What each act requires of areas and enemies

Input to the area and enemy tickets, not a roster.

| Act | Areas | Enemies | Required room |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2-3 test-facility areas | constructs and test harnesses | a combat-free mainframe room |
| 2 | 2-3 failing-city areas | repurposed civic machines and corporate hunter drones | a hub where the steward choice plays out |
| 3 | 2 areas of the hosted world | feral autonomous machines and corporate responses | a calm, combat-free space for the twist evidence |

Each act ends with a guardian or set-piece encounter. Area boundaries, count, sequence, and rosters belong to the area and encounter tickets.
