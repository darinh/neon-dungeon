NEON DUNGEON — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-21

What we collect

We collect anonymous gameplay telemetry to improve the game. This includes:

  • Device type (mobile, desktop, or tablet — no exact model or browser version)
  • Gameplay events: floors reached, upgrades chosen, weapons used, run outcomes
  • Performance data: frame rate, enemy/particle counts (sampled every 10 seconds)

What we do NOT collect

  • No personal information (no name, email, IP address, or account)
  • No browser fingerprinting (no user agent string, no screen dimensions)
  • No cross-session tracking (each page load is independent)
  • No third-party advertising or marketing data

Cookies

This game does not set any cookies. Analytics use in-memory storage only. When you close the tab, the session is forgotten.

Analytics provider

We use PostHog (US cloud) to process anonymous events. PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

Local storage

The game uses your browser's localStorage to save your game progress, settings, and a buffer of unsent telemetry events. This data stays on your device and is never shared except as described above.

Your rights

Since we collect no personal data and set no cookies, there is nothing to opt out of or delete. If you want to prevent telemetry entirely, you can block the domain us.i.posthog.com in your browser or use an ad blocker.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/darinh/neon-dungeon.
