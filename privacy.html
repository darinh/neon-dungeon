NEON DUNGEON — Privacy

Here's the deal: we don't care who you are.

We don't collect your name, your email, your location, or anything that identifies you. We don't set cookies or use advertising trackers. Analytics use a random session id, and unsent gameplay events may stay in this browser briefly so they can retry later.

So what DO we collect?

Anonymous gameplay stats. Things like:

  • Which floor you died on (and what killed you)
  • Which weapons and upgrades people actually pick
  • Whether anyone uses the Fusion Sword (we have opinions)
  • Frame rate and performance, so we know if something's lagging

That's it. No personal info. No fingerprinting. Just "did people like this thing or not."

Why?

Because we want to make the game better, and guessing doesn't work. If nobody picks a weapon, maybe it needs a buff — or maybe it needs to go. If everyone dies on floor 6, maybe the boss is overtuned. We can't fix what we can't see.

The technical bits

  • No cookies. Zero.
  • No account or identity tracking. Every page load gets a random analytics session id that is not tied to your save file, name, email, location, or account.
  • Retry queue: anonymous gameplay events are queued under neon_telemetry in this browser's localStorage before they are handed to the analytics transport. The queue is capped at the newest 2,000 events and is cleared after the configured analytics transport accepts the batch.
  • Events are sent to PostHog (US cloud) when available. Their privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.
  • Your save data (progress, settings) lives in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device.

Want to opt out?

Block us.i.posthog.com and us-assets.i.posthog.com in your browser or use any ad blocker. The game works exactly the same without it; blocking those endpoints prevents PostHog upload, though any available analytics stub may still accept and clear the local retry queue.
